A NOTE OF GRATITUDE

For the people who help make music possible.

A sincere thank-you to SUNO and everyone whose work helps make AI-powered music creation possible.

For independent creators, these tools open doors that once felt out of reach. An idea can become a melody, a memory can become a song, and words that lived only in someone's mind can find their way into the world.

Every Lamer & Buddy track begins with a story, a lyric, an experiment, or a feeling worth expressing. SUNO gives us another way to explore those ideas, discover new sounds, and share the results.

Our music comes from many places: imagination and strange cosmic adventures, laughter and late-night projects, and the real struggles that shape a life. The technology helps us bring those ideas to sound; the stories and the reasons for telling them are our own.

To the SUNO team, its developers, and everyone contributing to this technology: thank you for helping independent creators turn imagination into music.

These are our stories. This is our music. We are grateful for another way to share it.

LAMER & BUDDY
